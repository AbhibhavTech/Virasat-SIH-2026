⚠️ The Problem in Heritage Tourism

  • ❌
    Commercial LLM Hallucinations: Standard chatbots routinely invent non-existent monuments, incorrect entry timings, and fabricated ticket prices.
  • ❌
    Fragmented Multimodal Transit: Navigating between Indian Railways, Metro lines, suburban locals, MSRTC buses, and auto-rickshaws lacks unified regulated tariff calculation.
  • ❌
    Silent Monument Decay: Over-tourism and defacement go unrecorded without an accessible channel for citizens to report conservation emergencies directly to authorities.

✨ The Virasat Solution

  • ✅
    Zero-Hallucination Grounded AI: Every fact is strictly grounded against official Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) & UNESCO records with checkable citations.
  • ✅
    Regulated Multimodal Fare Engine: End-to-end door-to-door transit planning unifying suburban local trains, metro networks, state buses, and regulated meter autos.
  • ✅
    Citizen Stewardship & Health Scoring: Geo-validated damage and conservation reporting with automated destination health score degradation and administrative resolution queues.
