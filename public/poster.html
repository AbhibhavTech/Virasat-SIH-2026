⚠️ The Problem in Indian Heritage Tourism

  • ❌
    Generic AI Hallucinations: Standard LLMs invent non-existent monuments, invalid ticket prices, and fictional history.
  • ❌
    Last-Mile Transit Disconnect: Navigating between Indian Railways, Metro, state buses, and local autos lacks verified regulated fare benchmarks.
  • ❌
    Monuments Decay in Silence: Citizens lack real-time digital channels to report defacement, waste, or structural conservation emergencies to authorities.

✨ The Virasat Solution

  • ✅
    Grounded AI Concierge: Every fact is anchored to field-verified Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) & UNESCO records with strict citation provenance.
  • ✅
    True Multimodal Transit Engine: Seamless door-to-door routing unifying suburban trains, metro lines, MSRTC buses, and regulated meter autos.
  • ✅
    Citizen Stewardship & Health Scoring: Crowdsourced geo-tagged damage reporting with automatic destination health score decay and administrative resolution.
